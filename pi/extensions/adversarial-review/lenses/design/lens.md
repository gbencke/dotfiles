# design lens

Always applies. Reviews code against the structural principles of John
Ousterhout's *A Philosophy of Software Design* (2nd ed.). Scope: the
design/complexity chapters. Error handling is the `error-handling` lens's
ground (book ch10); comment accuracy is the `docs` lens's ground (ch12–13) —
do NOT double-report those here.
